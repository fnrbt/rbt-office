Word Render/Layout Parity Fixes

Focused regression table for DOCX render/layout parity work, newest fixes first. Each row keeps the real Word reference, our saved pre-fix rendering, and the current post-fix rendering. The frames are cropped into the problem area rather than full-page screenshots. Baseline images live under artifacts/baseline/ and are intentionally not committed.

About the numbers. Rows below the wrapSquare-float entries were authored before tools/word-parity/pixel-diff.py existed, so their captions describe visual approximation rather than measured pixel parity. Existing rows are still 90–99% ink-XOR vs Word at the pixel level — that's structural-match parity, not glyph-match parity. The recent pt→px import-side conversions (commits 0be7e1a, e3e716c, bde4a98) shift everything noticeably; sampling four repros showed the PDF backend's ink-XOR vs Word drops by 11–24 points on table/paragraph rows while browser stays roughly flat. To re-measure any repro: tools/word-parity/measure-parity.sh <repro-name>.
